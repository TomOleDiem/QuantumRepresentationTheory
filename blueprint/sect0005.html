4 Angular momentum

4.1 The algebraic representation

Definition 45 Angular momentum representation
#

Uses

  • Definition 7

Lean declarations

  • QuantumRepresentationTheory.AngularMomentumRepresentation

An algebraic angular momentum representation is an explicit representation \(\rho \) (Definition 7) of the Lie algebra \(\mathfrak {so}_3 \cong \mathfrak {sl}_2\) on a state space \(V\), presented via self-adjoint operators \(J_x, J_y, J_z\) satisfying \([J_x,J_y] = iJ_z\) and cyclic permutations.

Theorem 50 Spin classification
#

Uses

  • Theorem 49
  • Theorem 30

Lean declarations

  • QuantumRepresentationTheory.AngularMomentumRepresentation.spin_classification

Every finite-dimensional irreducible angular momentum representation is isomorphic to \(V(2j)\) for a unique half-integer spin \(j \in \tfrac 12\mathbb {N}\), of dimension \(2j+1\).
