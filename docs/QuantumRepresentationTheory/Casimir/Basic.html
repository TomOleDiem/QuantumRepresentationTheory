    Module.End K V

    Basis-dependent Casimir element (def:cas-basis-dependent). For a representation ρ of L on V, a basis b of L, and its Φ-dual basis (LinearMap.BilinForm.dualBasis), the Casimir operator is C_ρ = ∑ᵢ ρ(bᵢ) ρ(bⁱ).
